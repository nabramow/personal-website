Now building

social media was supposed to help us hang out. building the app that actually does.

serendipitymaxxing in app form: engineering more of the happy accidents that turn strangers into friends.

Born from moving to London and drowning in large WhatsApp communities and groups with zero actual plans. The research is blunt: ~50 hours together makes casual friends, 200+ makes close ones, and scrolling doesn’t count. you coming? turns “we should hang out” into plans that happen.

Product Engineer · Core Leadership Team · Board

A non-transactional, nonprofit couch-surfing platform built by volunteers: community and cultural exchange instead of ads and engagement bait. I designed and built the core map search that 90k+ members use to find each other, and I keep a scattered volunteer team shipping. 501(c)(3), used worldwide, and fully open source.

What I care about
  • Technology should give people back their time and attention, not compete for it. I want to build things that help you live your life, not pull you away from it.
  • I care a lot about who I build for. I want to work on things that make the world better, or at least aren’t quietly making it worse: environmentally, socially, politically, all of it.
  • The stuff I genuinely care about: health, eco-friendliness, public transit, and anything that gets people together in real life.
  • I’m ambitious from 9 to 5, and I believe ambition can apply to your personal life too.
