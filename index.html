My technical babies

you coming?

Live on iOS & Android

Social media was supposed to help us hang out. you coming? actually does.

serendipitymaxxing in app form: engineering more of the happy accidents that turn strangers into friends.

Born from moving to London and trying to build community in chatty WhatsApp groups where the actual plans kept getting drowned out. Research is clear: ~50 hours together makes casual friends, 200+ makes close ones, and scrolling doesn’t count. you coming? turns “we should hang out” into plans that happen. Now on the App Store and Google Play.

Couchers.org

Product Engineer · Core Leadership Team · Board

no product manager, no designer. I work out the user flows, get the team bought in, then build them.

A non-transactional, nonprofit couch-surfing platform built by volunteers: community and cultural exchange instead of ads and engagement bait. 501(c)(3), used worldwide, fully open source.

I rebuilt the map search that ~100k members use to find each other. And I shipped our iOS and Android app in React Native with Expo: 12k+ downloads so far, and mobile is now half of all traffic. Both started as rough flows I sketched and took to the team before writing code.

I’ve also cleared years of frontend tech debt, broken the worst components into pieces that can actually be tested, and set up ways of working that suit a volunteer team spread across timezones.

Impressive things I did at rad companies for cash monies

Articulate, the e-learning platform 125k organizations use for workplace training, including every company in the Fortune 100. I was on the team behind their AI localization product, where I built the proof of concept for the in-house translation API and saw it through to millions in ARR. I also designed and built its glossary infrastructure: it keeps brand-critical terms consistent at scale, and customers name it in their case studies.

Hopkins, legaltech making legal help more accessible, where I was the second full-time engineer hired. I built the client portal thousands of people use to follow their own cases, and the permissions system that let the company take on partner lawyers and legal insurers. The product is now a SaaS in its own right, Caseflow, and Hopkins went on to make the Financial Times’ list of Europe’s fastest-growing companies for 2026.
